## 2. Issues

### Critical

**A-001 — `/reset` loads three third-party origins.** [RULE]
Pages: `/reset`. reset.html must load no third-party script at all.
Evidence: at runtime it requests `browser.sentry-cdn.com`, `fonts.googleapis.com`
and `fonts.gstatic.com` (`audits/raw/2026-09-21/checks/reset.json`). Google Fonts
comes from `public/reset.html:19-21`; Sentry is injected by
`public/lumen-app.js:30` (`https://browser.sentry-cdn.com/10.73.0/bundle.min.js`),
which reset.html loads.
Fix: self-host Inter on reset.html and skip the Sentry loader there. Effort: S.

**A-002 — Inter is fetched from Google Fonts on 21 of 22 pages.** [RULE]
Pages: every page except `/`. The rule is that Inter is served from Lintel.
Evidence: `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter…">` in all
21 files (e.g. `public/about.html:15-16`); index.html instead preloads
`/fonts/inter/inter-latin-wght-normal.woff2` (`public/index.html:19`), which
serves 200. This is also a render-blocking stylesheet on those pages and the
likely cause of their 2.7s LCP against the homepage's 1.4s.
Fix: copy index.html's self-hosted `@font-face` block to the other 21 pages.
Effort: M.

**A-003 — "credit"/"credits" appears in page copy on five pages.** [RULE]
Pages: `/blog-no-credits.html` (7), `/about` (3), `/` (3), `/blog` (2), `/help` (1).
Evidence: `public/about.html:134,138,144`, `public/blog-no-credits.html:3-12,148,150-151`.
Note: the blog post is a deliberate argument against credit pricing, and the
homepage line is "no credits counting down". The rule as written admits no
exception, so this is reported as a failure for you to judge — either exempt
editorial copy or reword.
Fix: decide the exemption, then reword what remains. Effort: S.

### Major

**A-004 — Footer text fails contrast at 3.55:1.**
Pages: all (token is defined in all 22 files). Evidence: axe `color-contrast`,
`#5D6875` on `#05070C`, needs 4.5:1 — 18 nodes at 14.5px and 2 at 13px on `/`
(`.foot-brand > p`, `.foot-col` links, `.foot-bottom > span`), 2 nodes each on
`/start`, `/builder`, `/dashboard`, `/reset`, 1 on `/work`.
Fix: lighten `--grey-dim` to about `#8A94A3` for 4.5:1 on `#05070C`. Effort: S.

**A-005 — No caching on static assets.**
Pages: all. Evidence: `/fonts/inter/inter-latin-wght-normal.woff2`,
`/favicon-32.png`, `/lumen-app.js` and `/video/lintel-ad-poster.jpg` all return
`Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`
(`audits/raw/2026-09-21/headers.txt`). There are no hashed `/_next/static`
assets — the site is static HTML from `public/`, so nothing is cached at all.
Fix: serve `public/fonts`, `/video`, images and JS with
`max-age=31536000, immutable` via `vercel.json` headers. Effort: S.

**A-006 — The "Ask Ray" launcher covers content and controls.**
Pages: 20 of 23 at one or both widths. Evidence
(`audits/raw/2026-09-21/checks/extras.json`): fixed 121×45 pill, z-index 950,
bottom-right, present on all 22 pages via `public/ray.js`. It overlaps
interactive elements — "Sign in" on `/start`, `/builder`, `/dashboard` at 375px;
a form input on `/contact`, `/start` at 1280px; "View live site ↗" on `/demos`;
"Start from this" on `/templates` — and body text on 12 more. It cannot be
dismissed; the close button exists only inside the opened panel.
Fix: raise the launcher above the content column, or hide it while a form or
card control is beneath it. Effort: M.

**A-007 — Horizontal overflow at 200% zoom.**
Pages: 20 of 22 (not `/start`, `/builder`, `/dashboard`). Evidence: at a 188px
viewport (the 200%-zoom proxy for a 375px screen) `documentElement.scrollWidth`
exceeds `innerWidth` (`checks/*.json`, `zoom188`). WCAG 1.4.10 expects no
horizontal scrolling at 320px CSS width.
Fix: audit fixed-width children and `min-width` in the shared layout. Effort: M.

**A-008 — Tap targets below 44×44px.**
Pages: all. Evidence: `/terms` 44 of 64 interactive elements, `/privacy` 43/62,
`/` 35/69, `/templates` 30/58 (`checks/*.json`, `taps`). Mostly footer and
in-body links at 14.5px with no padding.
Fix: give footer and inline links a minimum 44px hit area. Effort: M.

**A-009 — Security headers missing.**
Pages: all. Evidence: every response carries only
`Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=63072000`; no `Content-Security-Policy`, no
`X-Frame-Options`, no `Referrer-Policy`, no `X-Content-Type-Options`
(`headers.txt`, 28 URLs).
Fix: add the four headers in `vercel.json`. Effort: S.

**A-010 — Mobile LCP 2.7–3.8s on 21 pages.**
Pages: all but `/`. Evidence: scorecard above; `/builder` 3.8s, `/dashboard`
3.3s, `/start` and `/demos` and `/kit` 3.2s, the rest 2.7s, against the
homepage's 1.4s. Largely A-002 (render-blocking Google Fonts) plus A-005 (no
caching).
Fix: A-002 and A-005 first, then re-measure. Effort: M.

**A-011 — Header and footer have drifted apart.**
Pages: all. Evidence: 6 distinct header link-sets and 5 footer link-sets.
`/` links "Start building" to `/builder`; the 16 standard pages link it to
`/#pricing`; `404.html` alone adds `/#process`; `/start` and `/reset` show "Back
to home" instead of the sign-in pair. Footers: `/terms` omits `/acceptable-use`
and `/cookies`; the three blog posts omit `/templates`; `/` uses bare `#studio`
style hashes where others use `/#studio`.
Fix: single shared header/footer partial, or reconcile the 22 copies. Effort: M.

**A-012 — `cdn.jsdelivr.net` is not in the known third-party list.**
Pages: `/start`, `/builder`, `/dashboard`. Evidence: `checks/start.json` — the
Supabase browser client is loaded from jsDelivr (`public/lumen-app.js:18`).
Supabase is on the known list; jsDelivr as a delivery origin is not, and it is a
third party that can execute script on your auth pages.
Fix: either self-host supabase-js or record jsDelivr as an accepted origin.
Effort: S.

**A-020 — No back or breadcrumb path** from 18 of 22 pages (only `/404`,

**A-021 — Content outside landmarks.** axe `region` flags every page; the

**A-022 — Form semantics gaps.** `/help`'s search input has no `<label>` (1

not announced. Effort: M.

  judgement needing a real assistive-technology pass.
