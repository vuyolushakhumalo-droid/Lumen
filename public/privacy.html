3. How we use it

  • To provide the service, generate your sites and host what you publish
  • To manage your account, subscription and payments
  • To enforce usage limits fairly
  • To provide support and respond to you
  • To keep Lintel secure, prevent abuse and investigate misuse
  • To improve the service and understand how it is used
  • To meet our legal obligations, including tax and accounting
  • To send service messages (and, with your consent, occasional product updates)

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

4. Legal bases

  • Contract — to provide the service you have signed up for
  • Legal obligation — for tax, accounting and responding to lawful requests
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, and improve Lintel, balanced against your rights
  • Consent — for optional analytics cookies and any marketing email, which you can withdraw at any time

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; or provide it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Email vuyolushakhumalo@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
