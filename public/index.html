How it works

From a sentence to a live site.

You do it yourself, in the builder: describe it, watch it take shape, change what you like, then put it live.

01

Describe

Tell it what the site is for, in a sentence. Add more detail if you like, but one sentence is enough to start.

02

Watch it build

Sections appear in front of you as it works. A first version takes minutes.

03

Refine by asking

Change anything in plain English — the words, the layout, the colours — and see it update.

04

Publish

Put it live on your own address. Hosting and SSL are done for you.

05

Keep editing

Change it whenever you like, for as long as you stay subscribed.
